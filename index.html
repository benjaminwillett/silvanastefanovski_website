About the artist

Three decades at the easel

Silvana Stefanovski is a Melbourne painter who has been making work for around thirty years.

She studied visual arts at RMIT, and has kept painting at the centre of her life ever since. Two subjects run through the work: motherhood and identity — who we are to the people we raise, and who we remain to ourselves.

After many years in the city she moved to the Mornington Peninsula, where she now lives and works at Arthurs Seat. The move opened a third subject. Her recent paintings turn outward, exploring the bush, light and coastline of her new surrounds.

The studio

Arthurs Seat

Arthurs Seat is the highest point on the Mornington Peninsula, a forested ridge rising above Dromana and Port Phillip Bay, about an hour south of Melbourne. Stringybark, grass trees and long views over the water surround the studio — the setting for Silvana's most recent work.
